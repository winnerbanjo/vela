Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. How Vela Technologies collects, uses, and protects your personal and booking data.

1. Introduction & Scope

Vela Technologies (“Vela”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), formerly Nile Booking, operates the online booking platform at vela.ng and associated services. We are dedicated to maintaining the trust and confidence of our service providers (“Merchants”) and their clients (“Clients”).

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and applicable regulations.

2. Information We Collect

Merchant Information: When you register as a service provider, we collect your business name, personal name, email address, WhatsApp telephone number, physical address/city, service menu items, pricing, staff profiles, and bank settlement details.

Client Booking Information: When a client books an appointment via a Vela link, we collect the client’s full name, telephone number, email address, requested service, preferred appointment time, and payment/deposit verification data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To enable scheduling and manage appointment bookings between clients and merchants.
  • To send automated booking confirmations and reminder notifications via WhatsApp and SMS.
  • To process deposit collections and remit settlements directly to provider bank accounts.
  • To prevent fraud, verify transfer receipts, and ensure secure platform operations.
  • To deliver customer support and platform enhancement updates.

4. Payment Security & Financial Data

Vela does not store full credit card details or bank account passwords on our servers. All digital card payments and virtual accounts are securely processed through CBN-licensed Payment Solution Service Providers (including Paystack and Moniepoint) utilizing PCI-DSS Level 1 certified infrastructure.

5. Data Retention & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party advertisers. Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide booking services, comply with accounting and statutory tax laws in Nigeria, and resolve any disputes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, or request the deletion of your account and related booking records. You can exercise these rights anytime by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at:
