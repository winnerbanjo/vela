Help & Support

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about setting up your booking page, collecting deposits, and automating your appointments.

You receive a unique, personalized booking link (e.g. vela.ng/p/yourname). Add this link to your Instagram bio or send it directly on WhatsApp. Clients open the link on any smartphone, choose the service they want, select an open time slot, and pay their deposit upfront to confirm.

Yes. Vela was built specifically to sit cleanly in your Instagram bio or WhatsApp quick replies. When a client messages asking “Are you free Saturday?”, you simply send your Vela link.

You decide your deposit requirement per service (e.g. ₦5,000 flat or 50% upfront). Clients pay the deposit using cards, Moniepoint, or direct Nigerian bank transfer. Confirmed deposits are settled directly into your Nigerian bank account or payment wallet.

Because clients pay a deposit to secure their appointment, your time is protected. You can define your own cancellation policy (e.g., non-refundable if cancelled within 24 hours of appointment).

Yes! On the Growth plan, you can add up to 3 staff members, and on the Scale plan, you get unlimited team members. Each team member can have their own schedule, assigned services, and individual appointment calendar.

Quarterly billing gives you 3 uninterrupted months of reliable booking infrastructure without annoying monthly card renewal failures or transaction friction. It keeps pricing significantly cheaper than standard monthly SaaS tools.
