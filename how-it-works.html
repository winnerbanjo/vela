Simple 4-Step Process

From Instagram message to confirmed deposit.

See how easy it is to replace back-and-forth WhatsApp inquiries with a professional booking workflow that puts money in your account.

01

Set up your profile and services

Add your business name, logo, location (or mobile service radius), and list your services with prices, appointment duration, and required deposit percentages (e.g. 50% upfront). Connect your Nigerian bank account or Moniepoint/Paystack wallet.

⏱ Takes under 3 minutes
02

Add your link to WhatsApp & Instagram

Place your custom Vela link (e.g. vela.ng/p/studioteni) in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp Business profile, and quick replies. When clients ask "Are you free this weekend?", just send your link.

🔗 One link for all channels
03

Clients pick an open time & pay deposit

Clients open your link on their phone, pick the exact service they need, view your real-time available calendar, and pay their deposit via card or verified bank transfer. No more double-booking or slot holding without commitment.

💳 Instant payment confirmation
04

You get a confirmed appointment and payment

Both you and the client receive immediate confirmation via WhatsApp and email. The appointment is synced to your calendar, and automatic reminders are scheduled so clients show up on time.

🛡️ Zero no-show anxiety
